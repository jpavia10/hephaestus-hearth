# HEARTH-GROK-checklists_20261110-1702: room-by-room renovation checklists

Hephaestus Hearth · renovation projects (scope item 1). Written by Grok, 20261110-1702 UTC, for the Prometheus one-day reset (WS-HEARTH).

**124 ordered checkpoints** across 10 areas. Each row: phase (0 before work → 1 inspect → 2 decide → 3 rough-in → 4 close-up → 5 final), the trade, what to check, and a verbatim quote from the cited source (every quote machine-checked against the source text fetched today).

**How to read the code citations.** Section numbers are the **2021 International Residential Code (IRC)** model text, read on UpCodes (Colorado DFPC's adoption page, which carries the model IRC 2021 text); the official ICC chapter link is in the JSON (`official_url`). Your jurisdiction may enforce a different edition or local amendments (California enforces its own Residential Code, which is based on the IRC). Treat each line as *what to ask the contractor and inspector about*, then confirm against the edition your building department enforces. Not legal or engineering advice. No product prices.

Row shape (JSON) matches the Hearth task record in `hearth_gemini_schema` (`id`, `title`, `trade`, `done`, `order`), plus `room`, `phase`, `check`, `cite`, `url`, `official_url`, `quote`. Room names use the same vocabulary as the shipped 120 inventory categories.

## Whole House (9)

| # | Phase | Trade | Checkpoint | What to check | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 before work | general | **Find out the year the house was built (pre-1978 = assume lead paint)** | Record build year in Hearth house notes. If before 1978, any job that disturbs painted surfaces uses a lead-safe certified contractor or lead-safe DIY practices. — “If your home was built before 1978, it may have lead-based paint” | [US EPA, Lead-Safe Renovations for DIYers (RRP)](https://www.epa.gov/lead/renovation-repair-and-painting-program-do-it-yourselfers) |
| 2 | 0 before work | hazmat | **Test suspect materials before demolition (asbestos)** | Before tearing out old flooring, popcorn ceilings, duct wrap or insulation, have an accredited professional sample them. Undisturbed, intact material can be left alone. — “You are planning to remodel your home (remodeling can disturb building materials)” | [US EPA, Protect Your Family from Exposures to Asbestos](https://www.epa.gov/asbestos/protect-your-family-exposures-asbestos) |
| 3 | 0 before work | general | **Plan smoke-alarm upgrades whenever a permit is pulled** | Any permitted interior alteration triggers smoke alarms to new-construction locations for the dwelling. Budget the alarms into the first interior project, not the last. — “Where alterations , repairs or additions requiring a permit occur, the individual dwelling unit shall be equipped with smoke alarms located as required for new dwellings” | [2021 IRC R314.2.2 Alterations, Repairs and Additions](https://up.codes/viewer/colorado/irc-2021/chapter/3/building-planning) |
| 4 | 0 before work | general | **Plan CO-alarm upgrades on the same trigger** | Same permit trigger applies to carbon monoxide alarms (exterior-only work such as roofing/siding/windows is exempt). — “Where alterations , repairs or additions requiring a permit occur, the individual dwelling unit shall be equipped with carbon monoxide alarms located as required for new dwellings” | [2021 IRC R315.2.2 Alterations, Repairs and Additions](https://up.codes/viewer/colorado/irc-2021/chapter/3/building-planning) |
| 5 | 0 before work | general | **Locate and label the main water shutoff** | Find the main shutoff near where the water service enters; confirm it is accessible and fully opens/closes. Photo it into Hearth before any plumbing work. — “Each dwelling unit shall be provided with an accessible main shutoff valve near the entrance of the water service” | [2021 IRC P2903.9.1 Service Valve](https://up.codes/viewer/colorado/irc-2021/chapter/29/water-supply-and-distribution) |
| 6 | 0 before work | electrical | **Locate the service disconnect and panel; check working space** | Clear 36 in. of working space in front of the panel; record the main breaker rating on the label. — “the dimension of the working space in the direction of access to panelboards and live parts of other equipment likely to require examination, adjustment, servicing or maintenance while energized shall be not less than 36 inches (914 mm) in depth” | [2021 IRC E3405.2 Working Clearances for Energized Equipment and Panelboards](https://up.codes/viewer/colorado/irc-2021/chapter/34/general-requirements) |
| 7 | 0 before work | general | **Dry any water damage within 24-48 hours before closing walls** | If demolition exposes wet framing or drywall, dry it within 24-48 h; mold areas over ~10 sq ft need a remediation plan, not a DIY wipe. — “It is important to dry water-damaged areas and items within 24-48 hours to prevent mold growth” | [US EPA, A Brief Guide to Mold, Moisture and Your Home](https://www.epa.gov/mold/brief-guide-mold-moisture-and-your-home) |
| 8 | 0 before work | general | **Inspect floors, walls, ceilings, stairs and a sample of doors/windows** | Walk each room: open/close doors and windows, note fogged double-pane glass (broken seals), loose rails or wide baluster gaps. — “any window that was obviously fogged or displayed other evidence of broken seals” | [InterNACHI Home Inspection Standards of Practice §3.10](https://www.nachi.org/sop.htm) |
| 9 | 0 before work | carpentry | **Look for signs of foundation movement before cosmetic work** | Sheetrock cracks, out-of-square door frames and unlevel floors point to structure first; resolve before new finishes. — “observed indications of possible foundation movement, such as sheetrock cracks, brick cracks, out-of-square door frames, and unlevel floors” | [InterNACHI Home Inspection Standards of Practice §3.3](https://www.nachi.org/sop.htm) |

## Kitchen (18)

| # | Phase | Trade | Checkpoint | What to check | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 inspect | general | **Survey existing kitchen: shutoffs under sink and at dishwasher** | Each fixture/appliance supply needs its own accessible shutoff; note any missing under the sink, at the dishwasher and fridge ice line. — “Shutoff valves shall be required on each fixture supply pipe to each plumbing appliance and to each plumbing fixture other than bathtubs and showers” | [2021 IRC P2903.9.3 Fixture Valves and Access](https://up.codes/viewer/colorado/irc-2021/chapter/29/water-supply-and-distribution) |
| 2 | 1 inspect | electrical | **Test countertop receptacles for GFCI protection** | Press TEST/RESET on every countertop receptacle (or its upstream GFCI). Any countertop outlet without GFCI protection is a fix item. — “125-volt through 250-volt receptacles that serve countertop surfaces and are supplied by single-phase branch circuits rated 150 volts or less to ground shall have ground -fault circuit-interrupter protection for personnel” | [2021 IRC E3902.6 Kitchen Receptacles](https://up.codes/viewer/colorado/irc-2021/chapter/39/power-and-lighting-distribution) |
| 3 | 1 inspect | electrical | **Check receptacles within 6 ft of the sink** | Every receptacle within 6 ft of the sink bowl edge needs GFCI protection (includes non-countertop ones). — “125-volt through 250-volt receptacles that are located within 6 feet (1829 mm) of the top inside edge of the bowl of the sink” | [2021 IRC E3902.7 Sink Receptacles](https://up.codes/viewer/colorado/irc-2021/chapter/39/power-and-lighting-distribution) |
| 4 | 1 inspect | plumbing | **Check for leaks and functional drainage at the sink** | Run water, fill and drain the sink; look for active leaks in the cabinet base. — “active plumbing water leaks that were observed during the inspection” | [InterNACHI Home Inspection Standards of Practice §3.6](https://www.nachi.org/sop.htm) |
| 5 | 2 decide | general | **Decide layout: countertop runs and island/peninsula** | Count every countertop wall space 12 in. or wider; each needs receptacles so no point is more than 24 in. from one. Lock the layout before electrical rough-in. — “Receptacle outlets shall be installed so that no point along the wall line is more than 24 inches (610 mm), measured horizontally, from a receptacle outlet in that space” | [2021 IRC E3901.4.1 Wall Spaces](https://up.codes/viewer/colorado/irc-2021/chapter/39/power-and-lighting-distribution) |
| 6 | 2 decide | electrical | **Plan at least two 20-A small-appliance circuits** | Confirm with the electrician that countertop/dining receptacles are on two or more 20-A circuits. — “A minimum of two 20-ampere-rated branch circuits shall be provided for all receptacle outlets specified by Section E3901.3” | [2021 IRC E3703.2 Kitchen and Dining Area Receptacles](https://up.codes/viewer/colorado/irc-2021/chapter/37/branch-circuit-and-feeder-requirements) |
| 7 | 2 decide | hvac | **Decide range hood: ducted to outdoors, rated airflow** | Ducted hoods must exit outdoors (never attic/crawl space) through a smooth, airtight duct with a backdraft damper. — “Domestic cooking exhaust equipment shall discharge to the outdoors through a duct” | [2021 IRC M1503.3 Exhaust Discharge](https://up.codes/viewer/colorado/irc-2021/chapter/15/exhaust-systems) |
| 8 | 2 decide | hvac | **Check minimum kitchen exhaust rate** | Pick a hood that meets at least 100 cfm intermittent (or 25 cfm continuous). — “Kitchens 100 cfm intermittent or 25 cfm continuous” | [2021 IRC M1505.4.4 Local Exhaust Rates](https://up.codes/viewer/colorado/irc-2021/chapter/15/exhaust-systems) |
| 9 | 2 decide | hvac | **Large hood (>400 cfm) with non-direct-vent gas appliances = makeup air** | If the hood exceeds 400 cfm and the house has atmospheric gas appliances, plan makeup air. — “each exhaust system capable of exhausting in excess of 400 cubic feet per minute (0.19 m 3 /s) shall be mechanically or passively provided with makeup air” | [2021 IRC M1503.6 Makeup Air Required](https://up.codes/viewer/colorado/irc-2021/chapter/15/exhaust-systems) |
| 10 | 2 decide | hvac | **Hood duct material** | Specify galvanized steel, stainless or copper duct for the hood run (PVC only for qualifying downdraft under slab). — “Ducts serving domestic cooking exhaust equipment shall be constructed of galvanized steel, stainless steel or copper” | [2021 IRC M1503.4 Duct Material](https://up.codes/viewer/colorado/irc-2021/chapter/15/exhaust-systems) |
| 11 | 3 rough-in | electrical | **Dishwasher circuit GFCI** | Verify the dishwasher branch circuit/outlet is GFCI protected. — “Ground -fault circuit-interrupter protection shall be provided for outlets supplied by branch circuits rated 150 volts or less to ground that supply dishwashers” | [2021 IRC E3902.11 Kitchen Dishwasher Branch Circuit](https://up.codes/viewer/colorado/irc-2021/chapter/39/power-and-lighting-distribution) |
| 12 | 3 rough-in | electrical | **AFCI on kitchen 15/20-A outlet circuits** | Kitchen outlet circuits need AFCI protection (combination AFCI breaker or approved method). — “Branch circuits that supply 120-volt, single-phase, 15- and 20-ampere outlets installed in kitchens” | [2021 IRC E3902.20 Arc-Fault Circuit Interrupter Protection](https://up.codes/viewer/colorado/irc-2021/chapter/39/power-and-lighting-distribution) |
| 13 | 3 rough-in | plumbing | **Dishwasher backflow: air gap or approved preventer** | Confirm an air gap (counter or integral) or backflow preventer on the dishwasher supply. — “The water supply to a dishwasher shall be protected against backflow by an air gap” | [2021 IRC P2717.1 Protection of Water Supply](https://up.codes/viewer/colorado/irc-2021/chapter/27/plumbing-fixtures) |
| 14 | 3 rough-in | plumbing | **Dishwasher drain high loop** | Drain hose/tube must rise to the underside of the counter before connecting to the disposer or tailpiece. — “The dishwasher discharge pipe or tubing shall rise to the underside of the counter and be fastened or otherwise held in that position” | [2021 IRC P2717.2 Sink and Dishwasher](https://up.codes/viewer/colorado/irc-2021/chapter/27/plumbing-fixtures) |
| 15 | 3 rough-in | plumbing | **Water hammer arrestors for quick-closing valves** | Dishwashers and ice makers use quick-closing valves; add arrestors at rough-in. — “A water-hammer arrestor shall be installed where quick-closing valves are utilized” | [2021 IRC P2903.5 Water Hammer](https://up.codes/viewer/colorado/irc-2021/chapter/29/water-supply-and-distribution) |
| 16 | 3 rough-in | plumbing | **Pressure-test new supply piping before closing walls** | Ask for the water-supply test (working pressure, or 50 psi air for non-plastic, held 15 min) before drywall. — “This pressure shall be held for not less than 15 minutes” | [2021 IRC P2503.7 Water-Supply System Testing](https://up.codes/viewer/colorado/irc-2021/chapter/25/plumbing-administration) |
| 17 | 4 close-up | electrical | **Countertop receptacle height** | Receptacles must be no more than 20 in. above the counter (or listed in-counter assemblies). — “On or above, but not more than 20 inches (508 mm) above, the countertop or work surface” | [2021 IRC E3901.4.3 Receptacle Outlet Location](https://up.codes/viewer/colorado/irc-2021/chapter/39/power-and-lighting-distribution) |
| 18 | 5 final | general | **Record new appliances into Hearth inventory** | Add range, hood, dishwasher, fridge, disposal with model/serial/receipt/warranty end (inventory categories 1-7). — “whenever you make a significant purchase, remember to add the information to your inventory” | [Insurance Information Institute, Home inventory brochure](https://www.iii.org/article/brochure-home-inventory) |

## Bathrooms (17)

| # | Phase | Trade | Checkpoint | What to check | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 inspect | plumbing | **Flush every toilet; check for rocking or leaks at the floor** | Loose or leaking toilets at the flange get fixed before new flooring. — “toilets that were damaged, had loose connections to the floor, were leaking, or had tank components that did not operate” | [InterNACHI Home Inspection Standards of Practice §3.6](https://www.nachi.org/sop.htm) |
| 2 | 1 inspect | electrical | **Test bathroom receptacles for GFCI** | Every bathroom receptacle must be GFCI protected. — “125-volt through 250-volt receptacles installed in bathrooms and supplied by single-phase branch circuits rated 150 volts or less to ground shall have ground -fault circuit-interrupter protection for personnel” | [2021 IRC E3902.1 Bathroom Receptacles](https://up.codes/viewer/colorado/irc-2021/chapter/39/power-and-lighting-distribution) |
| 3 | 1 inspect | hvac | **Confirm exhaust fan vents outdoors (not into attic)** | Trace the fan duct in the attic; termination inside the attic is a defect. — “Exhaust air from bathrooms , toilet rooms and kitchens shall not discharge into an attic , crawl space or other areas inside the building” | [2021 IRC M1505.2 Recirculation of Air](https://up.codes/viewer/colorado/irc-2021/chapter/15/exhaust-systems) |
| 4 | 1 inspect | general | **Check tub/shower glass for safety glazing marks** | Glass within 60 in. of the standing surface at tubs/showers is a hazardous location; look for the tempered/safety etch. — “Glazing in walls , enclosures or fences containing or adjacent to hot tubs, spas, whirlpools, saunas, steam rooms, bathtubs, showers” | [2021 IRC R308.4.5 Glazing and Wet Surfaces](https://up.codes/viewer/colorado/irc-2021/chapter/3/building-planning) |
| 5 | 2 decide | hvac | **Ventilation: window or fan sized to code** | Without a 3 sq ft window (half openable) you need a fan: 50 cfm intermittent or 20 cfm continuous. — “Mechanical exhaust capacity of 50 cfm intermittent or 20 cfm” | [2021 IRC M1505.4.4 Local Exhaust Rates](https://up.codes/viewer/colorado/irc-2021/chapter/15/exhaust-systems) |
| 6 | 2 decide | electrical | **Dedicated 20-A bathroom receptacle circuit** | Plan at least one 20-A circuit for bathroom receptacles with no other outlets. — “A minimum of one 20-ampere branch circuit shall be provided to supply bathroom receptacle outlet (s)” | [2021 IRC E3703.4 Bathroom Branch Circuits](https://up.codes/viewer/colorado/irc-2021/chapter/37/branch-circuit-and-feeder-requirements) |
| 7 | 2 decide | electrical | **Receptacle within 36 in. of each basin** | Place a receptacle within 36 in. of the outside edge of each lavatory basin. — “such outlet shall be located within 36 inches (914 mm) of the outside edge of each lavatory basin” | [2021 IRC E3901.6 Bathroom](https://up.codes/viewer/colorado/irc-2021/chapter/39/power-and-lighting-distribution) |
| 8 | 2 decide | plumbing | **Shower valve type: pressure-balance or thermostatic** | Specify a balanced-pressure, thermostatic or combination valve rated for the shower head flow. — “Individual shower and tub/shower combination valves shall be balanced-pressure, thermostatic or combination balanced-pressure/thermostatic valves” | [2021 IRC P2708.4 Shower Control Valves](https://up.codes/viewer/colorado/irc-2021/chapter/27/plumbing-fixtures) |
| 9 | 2 decide | plumbing | **Tub-only valve: temperature limit 120 F** | Tub (non-shower) valves need a temperature-limiting device capped at 120 F. — “equipped with a means to limit the maximum setting of the device to 120°F (49°C)” | [2021 IRC P2713.3 Bathtub and Whirlpool Bathtub Valves](https://up.codes/viewer/colorado/irc-2021/chapter/27/plumbing-fixtures) |
| 10 | 2 decide | general | **Ceiling height check before soffits or lowered ceilings** | Bathrooms need at least 6 ft 8 in. ceiling height. — “Bathrooms , toilet rooms and laundry rooms shall have a ceiling height of not less than 6 feet 8 inches (2032 mm)” | [2021 IRC R305.1 Minimum Height](https://up.codes/viewer/colorado/irc-2021/chapter/3/building-planning) |
| 11 | 3 rough-in | tile | **Tile backer board: listed material only (not plain drywall)** | Behind tub/shower tile use listed backers (cement board, fiber-cement, glass-mat gypsum, etc.). — “Materials used as backers for wall tile in tub and shower areas and wall panels in shower areas shall be of materials listed in Table R702.4.2” | [2021 IRC R702.4.2 Backer Boards](https://up.codes/viewer/colorado/irc-2021/chapter/7/wall-covering) |

| 13 | 3 rough-in | plumbing | **Shower riser anchored** | Supply riser from valve to head must be secured to framing. — “Water supply risers from the shower valve to the shower head outlet , whether exposed or concealed , shall be attached to the structure” | [2021 IRC P2708.3 Water Supply Riser](https://up.codes/viewer/colorado/irc-2021/chapter/27/plumbing-fixtures) |
| 14 | 3 rough-in | plumbing | **DWV test at rough-in** | Drain/waste/vent rough-in gets a water or air test without leakage before walls close. — “DWV systems shall be tested on completion of the rough piping installation” | [2021 IRC P2503.5.1 Rough Plumbing](https://up.codes/viewer/colorado/irc-2021/chapter/25/plumbing-administration) |
| 15 | 4 close-up | tile | **Nonabsorbent finish to 6 ft above the floor** | Shower walls and tub walls with a shower head: nonabsorbent finish at least 6 ft high. — “Such wall surfaces shall extend to a height of not less than 6 feet (1829 mm) above the floor” | [2021 IRC R307.2 Bathtub and Shower Spaces](https://up.codes/viewer/colorado/irc-2021/chapter/3/building-planning) |
| 16 | 4 close-up | electrical | **Receptacles near tub/shower stall** | Any receptacle within 6 ft of the tub or shower edge needs GFCI. — “located within 6 feet (1829 mm) of the outside edge of a bathtub or shower stall” | [2021 IRC E3902.8 Bathtub or Shower Stall Receptacles](https://up.codes/viewer/colorado/irc-2021/chapter/39/power-and-lighting-distribution) |
| 17 | 5 final | plumbing | **Fixtures secured and supported** | Toilet bolted to flange/floor with corrosion-resistant fasteners; wall-hung fixtures rigidly supported. — “Floor- outlet or floor-mounted fixtures shall be secured to the drainage connection and to the floor” | [2021 IRC P2705.1 General](https://up.codes/viewer/colorado/irc-2021/chapter/27/plumbing-fixtures) |

## Laundry (8)

| # | Phase | Trade | Checkpoint | What to check | Source |
