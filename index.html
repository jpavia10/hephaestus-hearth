Project · Second-floor deck

The deck rebuild

Field about 23 ft by 10 ft. 21 board courses confirmed — 20 remaining plus the one already removed on the railing side. Boards are 2x6x12 with staggered butt joints (alternating 12+11 and 5+12+6; on three-board rows the big board rides the middle and the smallest goes left), and every joint lands on framing — no floating ends. The deck lifts incrementally on two 3-ton floor jacks with temporary supports on solid foundations; no tarps — it is built in stages and waterproofed as it goes. Working blueprint: Revision 7.

Checking a stage with a target date keeps the Prometheus desk calendar honest — the date appears on the hub until the stage is done.
